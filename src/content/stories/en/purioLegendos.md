Legends of Jagannatha Puri is a documentary aired on the National Geographic channel. Rajeev Khandelwal will take us to Puri, Odisha state, to see the famous Rathayatra of Lord Jagannatha, Lord Balabhadra, and Devi Subhadra. Together with him, explore the magnificent architecture of the Jagannatha temple and learn how this Yatra brings together different cultures, religions, and mythology. The original series in English is available online at: [https://youtu.be/VI0RLlQ6dZg](https://youtu.be/VI0RLlQ6dZg)
