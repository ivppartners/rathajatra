---
title: "Krishna"
image: "../../../assets/images/jr.webp"
---

Sanskrit: The all-attractive one.

The Mahabharata, Bhagavata Purana, Bhagavad-gita, and other Vedic texts state that the Supreme Absolute Truth – Brahman – has many names and forms. However, His original form is revealed in Goloka Vrindavana of the spiritual world and is called by the name Krishna. He attracts all living beings by His beauty, strength, wealth, fame, wisdom, and impartiality.
