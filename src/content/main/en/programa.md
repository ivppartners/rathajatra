The Jagannatha Rathayatra festival will take place in Vilnius on Sunday, June 21, 2026.

The parade with the chariot begins at 12:00 PM at Lukiškės Square.

The concert program begins at 3:00 PM at Town Hall (Rotušės) Square.

12:00 Jagannatha with brother Baladeva and sister Subhadra arrives at the meeting place in Lukiškės Square. 

Arati - a ritual for the auspiciousness of all people;

Kirtan - chanting of Vedic mantras;

Peace Formula - a discussion on whether peace in the world is possible;

13:00 The chariot moves from Lukiškės Square. It travels along Gediminas Avenue, Šventaragio St., Pilies St. to Town Hall Square. Chanting of mantras and dancing take place while pulling the chariot.

15:00 The chariot arrives at Town Hall Square; Concert program where you can see traditional Indian Bharata Natyam dances and listen to Vaishnava devotional songs - bhajans.

Bharata Natyam is a classical Indian dance style originating from the Tamil Nadu region of South India. The name Bharata Natyam is composed of the words: bhava – emotion, raga – melody, tala – rhythm, and natyam – dance. It is a dynamic and highly precise style of dance, characterized by grace and power at the same time.

You can learn more about these dances at:

https://www.indiskisokiai.lt/indijos-sokis/

At Town Hall Square, you can enjoy delicious vegetarian dishes.

Afterwards, we all bid farewell to Jagannatha, Baladeva, and Subhadra to the Temple.
