June 21, 2026

Join one of the most colorful summer festivals – RATHAJATRA VILNIUS 2026!

This festival, originating from the ancient Indian bhakti yoga traditions, invites you to escape everyday life for a while and experience joy, music, community, and a uplifting spiritual atmosphere in the heart of the city.

"Cultural Diversity Enriches Us"
