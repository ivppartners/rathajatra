📅 June 21, 2026
📍 Festival begins – 12:00 PM at Lukiškės Square
📍 Cultural program – 3:00 PM at Town Hall Square

✨ Join one of the most colorful summer festivals – RATHAJATRA VILNIUS 2026! ✨

This festival, originating from the ancient Indian bhakti yoga traditions, invites you to escape everyday life for a while and experience joy, music, community, and a uplifting spiritual atmosphere in the heart of the city.

🎶 Colorful parade through Vilnius streets
💃 Indian dances and cultural performances
🎤 Ieva Zasimauskaitė, Edgaras Lubys, Inkilai, Kigidi Karma
🎵 Kirtans and chanting of holy names
🍛 Free vegetarian food
🌍 This year's theme – “Cultural diversity enriches us”

Mark your calendars and come experience the true spirit of the festival together! 🙏
